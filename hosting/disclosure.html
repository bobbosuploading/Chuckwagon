Affiliate disclosure

Last updated November 27, 2026

Chuckwagon is free to use. To help cover the cost of running it, some links in the app and on this website are affiliate links. That includes links to Walmart through the Walmart affiliate program on Impact, and may include other retailers in the future.

If you tap an affiliate link and buy something, Chuckwagon may earn a commission from the retailer. You pay the same price either way.

  • Affiliate relationships never decide which deals, stores or meals Chuckwagon shows you. Plans are built from the best prices we can find, whether or not a store pays us.
  • Prices and availability come from the retailer and can change. Always check the final price at checkout.
  • We don't post paid reviews or sponsored deals without labeling them.

This disclosure follows the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements. Questions? Email spring.ios.app@gmail.com.
