Features and how-to

Everything Chuckwagon does

Chuckwagon reads this week's grocery sales near you and turns them into meals, recipes and a shopping list. Here's every feature and where to find it in the app.

Getting started

  1. Install Apple's free TestFlight app, then open the beta link on your iPhone and tap Install.
  2. Open Chuckwagon and type your ZIP code, or tap Use my location. Stores near you show up, closest first, with how many deals each has this week.
  3. Tap the star next to the stores you shop at. They stay at the top under My stores.
  4. Open Settings (the sliders button) to set your household size, diet and allergies, and how far to look for stores (10 to 50 miles).
  5. Optional: in Settings, Sign in with Apple so your plans follow you to a new phone. You need it to share a household list or link Bloom Circle.

Meal plans from this week's sales

Tap Build a meal plan at the bottom of the store list or on any store's deals screen. Pick how many meals, how many people, and a weekly budget if you want one. Tap Plan my meals and you'll watch each meal appear as it's picked.

Up to 3 stores

Under Shop at, turn on up to three stores. Each item comes from whichever store has it cheapest, and your grocery list is split by store.

Recipes and a list

Every meal comes with step-by-step recipes. The grocery list shows what each line costs, the sale price, and about how much you save.

Swap meals

Each plan includes two spare meal ideas. On the Meals tab, tap the circles to swap a meal you don't want for a spare. The list and total update right away.

Chef's pick

Turn on Chef's pick for more creative, restaurant-style meals from a stronger AI cook. These take a few minutes, and they're a premium extra.

Stock-up tips

When a deal is good enough to buy extra (like meat that freezes well), the plan says so, and the tip comes along to your shopping list.

My plans

Every plan you make is saved under My plans, along with meals people have shared with you.

Cooking what you like

Moods and theme nights

Under In the mood for, tap quick picks like Mexican, Seafood, Italian or International, or type your own, like "something spicy". Turn on Pick a theme for each night to set one per meal.

Keepers

On a plan, swipe right on a meal and tap Keeper (the star). Keepers come first when you tap Pick saved meals to put old favorites into a new plan.

Feed it to the dogs

Swipe left on a meal you didn't like and tap Feed it to the dogs. It, and meals like it, stop showing up. Undo either list in Settings, under Food.

Cook's notes

Open a meal and tap Add a note under Cook's notes, like "twice the garlic". Your note shows whenever the meal comes back, and new plans cook it your way. Only you see your notes.

Plan styles

Cheapest possible

Under Plan style, turn on Cheapest possible for the lowest cost per meal, even if the meals are simple. Your diet and allergies always count.

Long cooks

Add 1 or 2 low-and-slow meals like smoked ribs, brisket or pulled pork, and pick smoker, slow cooker or either. They're marked "best on a weekend", with when to start, and leftovers often become another meal.

Cook from what you have

On Build a meal plan, turn on Cook from what I have and check the food you want used up (it lists what's on your on-hand list, or type it in). Then choose a trip:

  • Full trip: a normal plan that uses up your food first.
  • Few items: buy only the few things missing, on sale where possible.
  • No trip: cook only from what you already have.

Anything on your on-hand list stays off your grocery list, even on a normal plan.

Shopping list and on-hand list

One shopping list

Tap the checklist button at the top of the store list. Type anything you need, like milk or paper towels, and tap the circle to check it off. On a plan's grocery list, tap Add to shopping list to bring its items over without doubles.

Shared with your household

Sign in with Apple, tap Share this list, and send the link. Once they join, you both see one list, and changes show on both phones within a second or two.

Barcode scanning

Tap the barcode button and point the camera at a barcode, or pick a photo of one, to add the exact product. If the camera won't read it, type the numbers under the bars and tap Look up. If we can't name it, type it once and your phone remembers.

On hand at home

On the shopping list, tap On hand at home to track what's in your fridge, freezer and pantry, with amounts. Home from the store? Tap Move to on hand to move checked items over. Tap the flame on anything to cook soon. It's shared with your household too.

Keep the screen on

Your iPhone won't lock while a list, plan or recipe is open, so you don't need clean hands to unlock it. Pick how long in Settings, under Shopping and cooking.

Send to your store

Kroger lists can go straight to your Kroger cart (sign in to Kroger the first time). For other stores, tap Share list to send it, sorted by aisle, to Reminders, Notes or Messages.

Prices, stores and scanning

Kroger-family stores (Kroger, Fry's, Ralphs, King Soopers and more) give us live prices directly. H-E-B, Walmart, ALDI, Dollar General, Lowe's Market and others come from their weekly ads. Any other store can be added by scanning.

Scan a receipt

Tap Scan next to Build a meal plan and snap your receipt. We read the prices, tag items you got on sale, and each receipt earns you an extra plan that day.

Scan a weekly ad

Switch to Weekly ad and snap the pages, or pick up to 10 screenshots at once. The deals, with pictures cut from the ad, show for everyone at that store until the ad ends.

Add any store

Shopped somewhere that isn't listed? Pick Another store when scanning, and we add it near you with its prices.

Share a deal

See a grocery deal on Facebook or a deal site? Tap Share and pick Chuckwagon, or tap Share a deal on the home screen. It shows under "Spotted by shoppers" for that store.

Stock-up trip

Tap the box button on the home screen for bulk staples that cost less per pound at Sam's Club, with how much you save and how to store each one.

Sale dates

Each store's deals say how long the prices are good for, or when we last checked them.

Sale alerts and notifications

Everything here is off until you turn it on, in Settings, under Notifications.

Watch list

Tap Sale alerts and pick cuts to watch, like pork belly, brisket, ribs or chuck roast, or type your own, even toilet paper. You'll hear when one drops well below its usual price. You choose how big a drop is worth an alert.

Make it yourself

Turn on homemade bacon, ground beef or beef jerky, and we'll tell you when a cut on sale makes it cheaper to make your own than buy it, after trim and drying. Ground beef checks chuck, brisket, sirloin and round.

New deals

Turn on New deals at my stores to hear when a new weekly ad is in at your starred stores. At most one a week.

Plan reminder

Pick a day and time for a weekly "time to plan this week's suppers" nudge.

Tap See what's on sale now under Sale alerts to see what this week's ads would send you.

Sharing meals and plans

On a plan, tap the share button at the top right to send the whole plan and grocery list, or open a meal and tap share to send one recipe. When someone taps the link it opens in their Chuckwagon app, or on a web page if they don't have it yet. Meals shared with you are saved under My plans, in Shared with you.

Nutrition and Bloom Circle

Every meal shows calories, protein, carbs and fat per serving, with the full list (including fiber) when you open it. Numbers come from USDA food data and are estimates.

Use the Bloom Circle fitness app? Sign in with Apple in both apps, and you can pick your Chuckwagon meals, macros included, right inside Bloom.
