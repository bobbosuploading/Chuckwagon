Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Chuckwagon is an iPhone app that turns grocery store sales into meal plans and shopping lists. This policy explains what information the app and this website collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

What we collect

  • Your account. The app signs you in with an anonymous ID. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive the Apple ID token Apple provides, which may include a name and an email address (or Apple's private relay address if you hide your email).
  • Your area. Your ZIP code and its rough map position, used to find nearby stores. If you tap "Use my location", your phone looks up your ZIP code with location accuracy of about a kilometer, one time only. We never track your location in the background.
  • Your settings. Your chosen stores, household size, budget, meal preferences, favorites, meals you've skipped, and recipe notes. We use these to build your meal plans.
  • What you make in the app. Meal plans, shopping lists, watch lists and on-hand lists. If you share a list or a meal with your household or by link, the people you share with can see it.
  • Receipts and ads you scan. When you scan a receipt or weekly ad, the photo is sent to our server to read the prices. We keep the item names and prices we read from it, not the photo. Prices from scans may be used to show deals to other shoppers at that store, without anything that identifies you.
  • Barcodes. Barcodes you scan are looked up to find the product name.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, we store a device token so we can send the alerts you asked for.
  • Store accounts you link. If you link a Kroger account to send items to your cart, we store the access token Kroger gives us for that purpose. We never see your Kroger password.
  • Usage counts. We count how many plans and scans each account makes so we can keep the app free and stop abuse.

We do not collect your contacts, health data, or payment information, and the app has no advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

Only to run Chuckwagon: building meal plans, showing deals and prices, keeping your lists in sync, sending alerts you turned on, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase hosts our database, sign-in, server code and this website.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that writes meal plans and reads scanned receipts and ads. It receives your plan settings or the scanned image for that request only.
  • Apple delivers notifications and handles Sign in with Apple.
  • Grocery retailers such as Kroger and Walmart, when you choose to link an account, open a product link, or send items to a cart. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.
  • Bloom Circle, a companion fitness app from the same developer, can read your recent meals and nutrition only if you connect it with the same Apple sign-in.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Affiliate links

Some product links go to retailers through affiliate programs such as Walmart's on Impact. When you tap one, the retailer and the affiliate network may use cookies or similar tools to record that you came from Chuckwagon so a commission can be credited. See our affiliate disclosure.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you use the app. Deal and price data expires as sales end. To have your account and data deleted, email us at the address below and we'll remove it within 30 days.

Children

Chuckwagon is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app.
