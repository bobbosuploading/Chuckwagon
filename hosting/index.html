Supper from this week's sales

Chuckwagon reads your grocery store's weekly deals and turns them into a week of meals, with recipes and a shopping list, so you spend less and still eat well.

Join the free beta

Chuckwagon is in beta on Apple TestFlight. It's free to use while we test it.

What Chuckwagon does

🥩

Meals from the sales

Pick your stores, household size and budget. Chuckwagon builds a meal plan around what's on sale this week, with step-by-step recipes and nutrition per serving.

🛒

One shopping list

Every ingredient lands on a shopping list grouped by store, with sale prices and estimated savings. Share the list with your household and check items off together.

🔔

Sale alerts

Keep a watch list of things you buy often and get a heads-up when they go on sale, plus stock-up tips when a good price comes around.

📷

Scan and share

Scan a barcode to add an item, or scan a weekly ad or receipt to fill in prices for stores that don't publish them online.

🍲

Cook what you like

Choose meal themes, mark favorites and meals you'd rather skip, and keep private notes on recipes. Plans learn from what your household likes.

💵

Compare stores

See regular and sale prices side by side across nearby grocery chains, including Kroger, Walmart, H-E-B and more, so you know where your money goes furthest.

How it works

  1. Tell Chuckwagon your ZIP code and which stores you shop at.
  2. Chuckwagon gathers this week's deals from those stores.
  3. Ask for a plan. You get meals, recipes and a shopping list built around the best prices.
  4. Shop the list in store, or send items to a supported store's online cart.
