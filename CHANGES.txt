"What to Test" box. Lines in [square brackets] are notes for us, not for testers.

it goes up, add a new "Build N" section at the top of this list.

Build 12 (0.1.0)
========================================
- New "On hand" list for food you already have. Open the shopping list and tap "On hand at home". Type what's in your fridge, freezer and pantry (with how much, like 3 lb), or tap the barcode button to scan it. It's shared with your household, just like the shopping list.
- Home from the store? Check items off on the shopping list, then tap "Move to on hand" by "In the cart" and they move over. Tap the flame on anything that needs cooking soon. Swipe left when something's used up, or right to put it back on the shopping list.
- Cook from what I have. On "Build a meal plan", turn on "Cook from what I have" and check the proteins and other food you want used up (or type them in). Then pick "Full trip", "Few items" (buy only the few things missing, on sale where we can) or "No trip" (cook only from what you have).
- Everything on your on-hand list stays off the grocery list, even on a normal plan.
- Cheapest possible. Under "Plan style", turn it on for the lowest cost per meal, even if the meals are simple. Only your diet and allergies count; moods and dislikes are skipped.
- Long cooks. Under "Plan style", add 1 or 2 low-and-slow meals like smoked ribs, smoked chicken, brisket or pulled pork, and pick smoker, slow cooker or either. They're marked "Long cook, best on a weekend", with times and when to start, and leftover meat often turns into another meal.
- Try it: add a few things to On hand, mark one with the flame, then build a plan with "Cook from what I have" and "No trip". Then try "Few items", and a normal plan with 1 long cook on the smoker.
Also in this build (from build 11, which wasn't uploaded on its own):
- The barcode scanner on the shopping list should now read barcodes. In build 10 it could show the camera without picking anything up. You'll feel a tap when it catches one. Hold the phone steady a few inches away. On a round bottle, turn it so the bars face you flat.
- Camera won't read it? Type the numbers printed under the barcode instead and tap "Look up", or pick a photo of it.
- Barcodes the app read but couldn't name (like some household cleaners) are looked up in more places now. Scan the same item again to check.
- Try it: scan a cleaner or snack you always buy and add it to your list.
- Your iPhone no longer locks while the shopping list, a meal plan or a recipe is open, so you don't have to unlock it with your hands full. Choose how long in Settings, under "Shopping and cooking": off, 30 minutes (the default), 1 hour, or as long as it's open.
- The keyboard on the shopping list goes away when you scroll, tap "Done" above it, check off an item, or tap return in an empty box. With an item typed, return adds it and keeps the keyboard up for the next one.
- "Stock up" tips from a meal plan (like "Freezes well. Buy extra") now come along when you tap "Add to shopping list".
[The lookup fix is in functions (barcodeProduct): needs a functions deploy, and works on build 10 too.]
[Stock-up tips need the new firestore rules deployed before build 11 is used, or "Add to shopping list" is refused.]
[Needs a deploy of firestore and functions (new households/{id}/onHand rules; planMealsForStore reads onHand, useUp, storeTrip, cheapest, longCooks, longCookHow; joining a household carries the on-hand list).]

Build 10 (0.1.0)
========================================
- Add your own notes to any recipe. Open a meal and tap "Add a note" under "Cook's notes" to write down what you changed to make it better, like "used twice the garlic" or "10 more minutes in the oven". Only you can see your notes.
- Your note shows under the meal's name whenever that meal comes up again: in new plans, in My plans, and in "Pick saved meals".
- New plans read your notes too, so a meal you've noted should come back cooked your way. Notes never override your allergies or diet.
- See, edit or delete all your notes in Settings, under Food: "Cook's notes".
- Try it: add a note to a meal, make it a Keeper, then start a new plan, tap "Pick saved meals", pick that meal, and check the new recipe follows your note.
[Needs a functions deploy (new saveMealNote callable; planMealsForStore reads users/{uid}.mealNotes and adds up to 12 notes to the prompt only when there are any).]
- Notifications, if you want them. In Settings, under Notifications, you can turn on "New deals at my stores" to hear when a new weekly ad is in at your starred stores (or the three closest, if you haven't starred any). You can also pick a day and time for a weekly "time to plan this week's suppers" reminder.
- Both are off until you turn them on, and you'll get at most one deals alert and one reminder a week. iPhone asks once if Chuckwagon may send notifications.
- Try it: turn one on, tap "Send a test notification", and check it shows up. Set the reminder for later today and check it comes at that hour. Deals alerts come the next time a store's weekly ad loads, during the day.
[Needs the Apple push key uploaded in the Firebase console (Project settings > Cloud Messaging) and a deploy of firestore and functions (saveNotificationSettings, sendTestNotification, hourly sendNotifications, new deals index on storeId + validTo).]
- Sale alerts. In Settings, under Notifications, tap "Sale alerts" to pick cuts to watch (pork belly, brisket, ribs, pork butt, chuck roast and more) or type in anything of your own, like toilet paper. You'll hear when one goes on a big sale compared with its usual price.
- Make it yourself: switch on homemade bacon, ground beef or beef jerky, and we'll tell you when a cut on sale makes it cheaper to make your own than to buy it, after trim and drying. Ground beef checks chuck, brisket, sirloin and round and picks the cheapest.
- You choose how big a saving is worth an alert (30% to start). We check once a day and send at most one sale alert every 6 days.
- Try it: switch on a few cuts and the make-it-yourself checks, then tap "See what's on sale now" to see what this week's ads would send you.
[Sale alerts need a functions deploy (checkSaleWatch callable; sendNotifications runs a daily watch check and writes watchPrices/{storeId}). Kroger price books gain 14 meat and jerky searches, about 14 more API calls per store per run. H-E-B usual prices improve as watchPrices history builds.]
- New shopping list you can share with your household. Tap the checklist button at the top of the store list. Type anything you need, like milk or paper towels, and tap the circle to check it off.
- On a meal plan's grocery list, tap "Add to shopping list" to put the plan's items on it. Items already on the list aren't added twice.
- To share it, sign in with Apple, then tap "Share this list" at the bottom and send the link to your spouse or family. Once they join, you both see one list. Anything either of you adds or checks off shows up on both phones within a second or two.
- Scan a barcode to add the exact product you buy, brand and size included. On the shopping list, tap the barcode button and point the camera at it, or pick a photo of the barcode. If we can't find the name, type it once and your phone remembers it next time. Try it on a cleaner or snack from your pantry.
- Try it with someone: share the list, have them open the link on their iPhone and join, then add and check off items on both phones.
[Needs a Firebase deploy of firestore, functions and hosting (new households rules, startHousehold / householdInviteLink / householdInviteInfo / joinHouseholdInvite / leaveHouseholdList, barcodeProduct, and the /h/ invite links). Until then the list shows an error.]

Build 9 (0.1.0)
========================================
- When a meal plan can't be made, you now see why in a message right away. Before, the "Rustling up supper" screen flashed and you were sent back to the form with no explanation (the reason was hidden at the very bottom of the form). The reason also stays at the top of the form after you tap OK.
- Fixed: "Plan my meals" could flash "Rustling up supper" and stop with "Couldn't reach Chuckwagon", even though your plan was still being made (it showed up later in My plans). Now the app keeps waiting and opens the plan when it's ready. Build a few plans, with and without Chef's pick, and check that each one opens.
- "Couldn't reach Chuckwagon" messages now end with a short code in brackets, like "(network -1004)" or "(server unavailable)". If you see one, send us a screenshot.
- Tap "Plan my meals" a few times in one day, with and without Chef's pick, and check that the message makes sense when you hit a limit.

Build 8 (0.1.0)
========================================
- Plans no longer say "You save $0.00" when a weekly ad doesn't print regular prices (Brookshire Brothers' ad, for example). We now estimate the regular price from everyday prices, so the plan says "You save about $X", and items show "save ~$X est.". If we can't find a price to compare, the plan shows "On sale $X" and says the ad didn't list regular prices. Scan a Brookshire ad, build a plan, and check that the savings look reasonable.
- On your grocery list, each item's price is now what you'll pay for the whole line (for example 2 lb of shrimp), so the prices add up to the total. Items we couldn't price say "no price".
- Recipe steps no longer show two numbers, like "1. 1. Cook the rice".
- Shared plans (from "Share this plan") now show line prices that add up to the total, say which items have no price, and no longer mention meal ideas you didn't pick. Share a plan and open the link in Messages or Safari to check.
[Share page and step fixes are in functions (sharePage, planMealsForStore); they reach old links and older builds after a functions deploy.]
- Meals now show nutrition per serving: calories, protein, carbs and fat. You'll see a short line under each meal, and open a meal for the full list with fiber. It can take a few seconds to show up on a new plan. Check whether the numbers look about right for your meals.
[Macros come from USDA FoodData Central via addPlanMacros. Needs a functions deploy, plus the USDA_API_KEY repo secret.]
- Rate your meals, chuckwagon style. On a plan's Meals tab, swipe right on a meal you'd cook again and tap "Keeper" (the star). Swipe left on one you don't like and tap "Feed it to the dogs". You can also press and hold a meal, or open it and use the buttons under "How was it?".
- Meals you feed to the dogs stop showing up in new plans, along with ones like them. If it was one of your picked meals, a spare idea takes its place.
- Keepers come first when you tap "Pick saved meals" on the plan screen. Type in the search bar to find any past meal, or turn on "Show all past meals". Meals you fed to the dogs are hidden there.
- See and undo both lists in Settings, under Food: "Keepers" and "Fed to the dogs".
- Try it: make two meals Keepers, then start a new plan and tap "Pick saved meals". Feed a meal you keep getting to the dogs, make a new plan for the same store, and check it doesn't come back.
[Needs a Firebase deploy of functions (new dislikeMeal and likeMeal; planMealsForStore reads users/{uid}.dislikedMeals). Until then Keeper and Feed it to the dogs show an error.]

Build 7 (0.1.0)
========================================
- There's now a "Scan" button next to "Build a meal plan" at the bottom of the store list. Tap it to scan a receipt or a weekly ad without opening a store first. It starts on your closest starred store, and you can pick any store near you or "Another store".
- Weekly ads: pick several pages from Photos at once (up to 10), in order. They count as one ad, so if only one page shows the dates the prices are good for, the other pages use those dates. Try it with screenshots of a store's ad website, like Brookshire Brothers, where the dates are only at the top.
- If none of the pages show dates, we use the dates from that store's ad someone scanned recently, or guess this week's ad (Wednesday to Tuesday). Guessed dates are marked "GUESSED" under "Sale dates". Tap "Change dates" to fix them for every page you scanned.
- Pages you scan one at a time with the camera also count as one ad until you leave the scan screen.
- Deals from a weekly ad you scan now show the product's picture, cut from the ad page. Scan an ad page and check that the pictures match the items.
- Deals without a picture show an icon for their kind of food (meat, produce, dairy and so on) instead of a basket.
- Tell us what you're in the mood for. On the plan screen, under "In the mood for", tap one or more quick picks like Mexican, Seafood, Italian or International, or type your own (for example "something spicy"). Your meals follow it and are still built from what's on sale.
- Want a theme night? Turn on "Pick a theme for each night" and choose one for each meal, like Mexican for meal 1 and Seafood for meal 3. Leave a night on "Any" to let us choose.
- Try a seafood night with a budget turned on, and try "International" to get a different cuisine each night. If the sales don't fit a theme well, the plan's note tells you.
- Each store's deals now say when the prices are good, right above the deals. Stores with a weekly ad (like H-E-B and ALDI) show "Sale prices good through" the ad's last day. Kroger, which doesn't give end dates, shows when we last checked its prices, like "Prices checked today".
- Open H-E-B and a Kroger store and check that the dates look right.
[Needs a Firebase deploy of functions (planMealsForStore reads theme and nightThemes). Until then the app's themes are ignored.]
[Scan button is on StoresHomeView.]
[Pictures need Firebase Storage turned on (production mode) and a functions deploy (scanAdPhoto, new cleanEndedAdScans). They work for older builds too, since the app already shows any picture a deal has.]
[Needs a Firebase deploy of functions (scanAdPhoto, new fixAdDates) before this build reaches testers. The backend part works for older builds too: their date-less pages now get borrowed dates instead of an error.]

- Share a meal or a whole plan: on a plan, tap the share button at the top right to send the meals and grocery list, or open a meal and tap share to send just that recipe. Send it by Messages, email or anything else. When the other person taps the link, it opens right in their Chuckwagon app. If they don't have the app, they see the recipe on a web page with a link to join the beta.
- Meals people share with you are saved under My plans, in "Shared with you", so you can find them again.
- Meal plans start a little faster.

- Everyday meal plans fail less often. Some used to stop with an error after about a minute of planning, especially with two stores and extra meal ideas.
- Picking fewer meals now clears out the groceries for the meals you dropped. Before, some items from dropped meals stayed on the list and in the total, so a one-meal plan could show 14 items. To fix a plan you already have, tap any meal on the Meals tab to drop it or add it back, and the list rebuilds.

Build 4 (0.1.0)
========================================
- The app now opens on a list of stores near you, closest first, with how far each one is and how many deals it has this week. Tap a store to see its deals and plan supper. Tap the star next to the stores you shop at to keep them at the top under "My stores". If a store has no deals yet, it names the closest store of that chain that does. To switch stores from the deals screen, tap the store's name, or go back to the list.
- New to the app? You start on that same screen: type your ZIP code or tap "Use my location" and your stores show up. Household size, diet and allergies are in Settings (the sliders button), and there's a shortcut to them at the bottom of the store list.
- Scan a weekly ad: the scan button now does receipts and printed weekly ads. Switch to "Weekly ad" and snap one page at a time, with the dates the prices are good for. The deals show up on the home screen and in your plans until the ad ends. Try it with a store that has no deals in the app, like ALDI or a local grocer: pick "Another store" and we'll add it near your ZIP code. Coupons can't be scanned yet.
- Share a deal: saw a grocery deal on Facebook, TikTok, Reddit or a deal site? In that app, tap Share and pick Chuckwagon (you may need to tap "More" the first time), or share a screenshot from Photos. Then open Chuckwagon, pick the store and tap "Add this deal". You can also tap "Share a deal" on the home screen and paste a link or the post's words. Shared deals show under "Spotted by shoppers" for everyone at that store. If you check one in the store, tap "Still there" or "Gone" so others know. Deals without an end date go away after a few days.
- Stock-up trip: tap the box button at the top of the home screen for a list of bulk staples at Sam's Club that cost less per pound than H-E-B and Kroger, like bacon, butter, shredded cheese, oats and olive oil. It shows how much cheaper each one is and how to store it. Check off what you want and share the list. Prices are from the Sam's Club website and are updated about once a month.
- Tap any deal on the home screen to see all of it: a bigger ad picture, the full name and deal text, the price and the store.
- The deals banner no longer says "Up to 0% off" when a store's deals are all "buy this, get that" offers.
- The "Stock up" button on the home screen now works on its own: it shows every stock-up price from all aisles, and picking an aisle like Meat turns it off.
- Scanning on a weak cell signal: receipt and ad photos are now smaller, so they upload faster, and the app waits longer before giving up. If a scan still fails, you'll see what to try (like Wi-Fi or better service) instead of an error code like "DEADLINE EXCEEDED". Try scanning a long receipt with one or two bars.

[Share a deal needs a Firebase deploy of functions (shareDealPost, voteSharedDealPost) and

group.com.bobby.chuckwagon, which Xcode adds when signing (see ios/README.md).]

What's new since the first build:

- Your location: tap "Use my location" or type a ZIP code to set your stores. Nearby stores share prices from one store in the same chain region.
- Budget-first planning: set a weekly budget and the plan stays under it.
- Stock-up tips: a heads-up when a deal is good enough to buy extra.
- Send to cart: Kroger adds items straight to your cart, Walmart gets an add-to-cart link, and H-E-B and the other stores get "Share list" (sorted by aisle) to Reminders, Notes or Messages.
- More stores and prices: Walmart, ALDI and Dollar General ads, plus everyday prices. All 31 H-E-B ad deals now show, tagged "DEAL" where no regular price is listed.
- Receipt rewards: each scanned receipt earns an extra plan, and 3 receipts earn a Chef's pick.
- Meal ideas to pick from: every plan now comes with 2 extra meal ideas. On the Meals tab, tap the circles to swap a meal you don't like for one of the extras, or add one. The grocery list and total update right away, and it doesn't use up a plan.
- Sale prices on receipts: when you scan a receipt, items bought on sale or with a coupon get a red SALE tag with the regular price. Sale prices no longer count as a store's everyday price.
- Store distance: in Settings, under "Your stores", pick how far to look for stores (10, 20, 30 or 50 miles). It starts at 30.
- Stores in nearby towns, like Kroger, now show up even when there isn't one in your own ZIP code.
- The store menu on the home screen now updates as soon as you close Settings, so stores you pick there show up right away.
- If you add a store under "Also stop at" and nothing there beats the other stores, the plan's notes now say so instead of leaving it off without a word.

Please try: setting your ZIP, making a plan with a budget, swapping a meal on the Meals tab, scanning a receipt, and sending a list to your store. Tell us about anything that looks wrong or confusing.
