CHUCKWAGON: WHAT'S NEW IN EACH TESTFLIGHT BUILD

Newest build first. Copy the lines under a build's heading into TestFlight's
"What to Test" box. Lines in [square brackets] are notes for us, not for testers.
The build number lives in ios/project.yml (CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION). Every time
it goes up, add a new "Build N" section at the top of this list.

========================================
Build 6 (0.1.0)
========================================
- "Headed to" is now "Build a meal plan". There's a big "Build a meal plan" button at the bottom of the store list and on every store's deals screen.
- At the top of the plan screen, under "Shop at", turn on the stores you want to shop at, up to 3, right next to meals, people, budget and the rest. From a store's deals screen, that store starts out picked. From the store list, the stores you picked last time start out picked.
- With more than one store picked, each item still comes from the store where it's cheapest, and your list is split by store. Try a plan with two stores, then one with three.
- The "Shop at" list shows your starred stores. If you haven't starred any, it shows every store near you.
- Build a plan around meals you already like: on the plan screen, under "Meals to include", tap "Pick saved meals" and check meals from your past plans or ones people shared with you. We keep your recipe, buy its ingredients on sale where we can, and plan the rest of the meals around it. Try picking one or two favorites with a budget turned on.
- Scan a receipt or a weekly ad from any store near you. The "Store" list on the scan screen now shows every nearby store, not just your starred ones.
- Shopped somewhere that isn't in the list, like Brookshire Brothers? Pick "Another store" and scan the receipt. We read the store's name from the receipt (or type it in) and add the store near you with its prices. Weekly ads work the same way.
- Lowe's Market's weekly ad now loads on its own for people near a Lowe's Market (East Bernard, Needville, Palacios and more).

Build 5 (0.1.0)
========================================
- Share a meal or a whole plan: on a plan, tap the share button at the top right to send the meals and grocery list, or open a meal and tap share to send just that recipe. Send it by Messages, email or anything else. When the other person taps the link, it opens right in their Chuckwagon app. If they don't have the app, they see the recipe on a web page with a link to join the beta.
- Meals people share with you are saved under My plans, in "Shared with you", so you can find them again.
- Meal plans start a little faster.
- Chef's pick plans no longer stop with "DEADLINE EXCEEDED". They can take a few minutes, so the app now waits for them, and if it does lose track of one, it checks for the finished plan and opens it. Try a Chef's pick plan with a second store turned on.
- When something does go wrong, you'll see what to try instead of an error code.
- While a meal plan is being made, you now see what step it's on, and each meal shows up as soon as it's picked, so you're not staring at a spinner for a minute. Try a Chef's pick plan, which takes the longest.
- Everyday meal plans fail less often. Some used to stop with an error after about a minute of planning, especially with two stores and extra meal ideas.
- Picking fewer meals now clears out the groceries for the meals you dropped. Before, some items from dropped meals stayed on the list and in the total, so a one-meal plan could show 14 items. To fix a plan you already have, tap any meal on the Meals tab to drop it or add it back, and the list rebuilds.
- The plan header says "1 meal" instead of "1 meals".

Build 4 (0.1.0)
========================================
- The app now opens on a list of stores near you, closest first, with how far each one is and how many deals it has this week. Tap a store to see its deals and plan supper. Tap the star next to the stores you shop at to keep them at the top under "My stores". If a store has no deals yet, it names the closest store of that chain that does. To switch stores from the deals screen, tap the store's name, or go back to the list.
- New to the app? You start on that same screen: type your ZIP code or tap "Use my location" and your stores show up. Household size, diet and allergies are in Settings (the sliders button), and there's a shortcut to them at the bottom of the store list.
- Scan a weekly ad: the scan button now does receipts and printed weekly ads. Switch to "Weekly ad" and snap one page at a time, with the dates the prices are good for. The deals show up on the home screen and in your plans until the ad ends. Try it with a store that has no deals in the app, like ALDI or a local grocer: pick "Another store" and we'll add it near your ZIP code. Coupons can't be scanned yet.
- Share a deal: saw a grocery deal on Facebook, TikTok, Reddit or a deal site? In that app, tap Share and pick Chuckwagon (you may need to tap "More" the first time), or share a screenshot from Photos. Then open Chuckwagon, pick the store and tap "Add this deal". You can also tap "Share a deal" on the home screen and paste a link or the post's words. Shared deals show under "Spotted by shoppers" for everyone at that store. If you check one in the store, tap "Still there" or "Gone" so others know. Deals without an end date go away after a few days.
- Stock-up trip: tap the box button at the top of the home screen for a list of bulk staples at Sam's Club that cost less per pound than H-E-B and Kroger, like bacon, butter, shredded cheese, oats and olive oil. It shows how much cheaper each one is and how to store it. Check off what you want and share the list. Prices are from the Sam's Club website and are updated about once a month.
- Tap any deal on the home screen to see all of it: a bigger ad picture, the full name and deal text, the price and the store.
- The deals banner no longer says "Up to 0% off" when a store's deals are all "buy this, get that" offers.
- The "Stock up" button on the home screen now works on its own: it shows every stock-up price from all aisles, and picking an aisle like Meat turns it off.
- Scanning on a weak cell signal: receipt and ad photos are now smaller, so they upload faster, and the app waits longer before giving up. If a scan still fails, you'll see what to try (like Wi-Fi or better service) instead of an error code like "DEADLINE EXCEEDED". Try scanning a long receipt with one or two bars.

What's new since the first build:

- Your location: tap "Use my location" or type a ZIP code to set your stores. Nearby stores share prices from one store in the same chain region.
- Budget-first planning: set a weekly budget and the plan stays under it.
- Stock-up tips: a heads-up when a deal is good enough to buy extra.
- Send to cart: Kroger adds items straight to your cart, Walmart gets an add-to-cart link, and H-E-B and the other stores get "Share list" (sorted by aisle) to Reminders, Notes or Messages.
- More stores and prices: Walmart, ALDI and Dollar General ads, plus everyday prices. All 31 H-E-B ad deals now show, tagged "DEAL" where no regular price is listed.
- Receipt rewards: each scanned receipt earns an extra plan, and 3 receipts earn a Chef's pick.
- Meal ideas to pick from: every plan now comes with 2 extra meal ideas. On the Meals tab, tap the circles to swap a meal you don't like for one of the extras, or add one. The grocery list and total update right away, and it doesn't use up a plan.
- Sale prices on receipts: when you scan a receipt, items bought on sale or with a coupon get a red SALE tag with the regular price. Sale prices no longer count as a store's everyday price.
- Store distance: in Settings, under "Your stores", pick how far to look for stores (10, 20, 30 or 50 miles). It starts at 30.
- Stores in nearby towns, like Kroger, now show up even when there isn't one in your own ZIP code.
- The store menu on the home screen now updates as soon as you close Settings, so stores you pick there show up right away.
- If you add a store under "Also stop at" and nothing there beats the other stores, the plan's notes now say so instead of leaving it off without a word.

Please try: setting your ZIP, making a plan with a budget, swapping a meal on the Meals tab, scanning a receipt, and sending a list to your store. Tell us about anything that looks wrong or confusing.
